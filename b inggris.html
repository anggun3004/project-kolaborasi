Cardboard Waste Observation

Waste is a side product/by-product resulting from a process carried out by humans. Waste materials can be recycled. They can be in the form of rubber, food scraps, cardboard, plastic, and so on. Waste is generally divided into two, namely organic and inorganic waste. This time, I will discuss cardboard waste.

Cardboard waste is a type of waste that is difficult to decompose, requiring months for it to break down. Even then, the cardboard waste must be in an environment exposed to oxygen. Cardboard waste can be found everywhere, such as at home, in markets, and elsewhere. Even though it is waste, it still has many benefits when recycled into useful goods.

Cardboard waste comes in various shapes such as cubes, rectangles, and others. The color of cardboard also varies, such as white, brown, and others. It has a texture that is stronger than paper. Corrugated board material consists of a flat layer and a corrugated layer in between, making it strong, rigid, yet light.

Cardboard waste has various uses. Cardboard waste can become useful items. For example, it can be made into a study/laptop table, cabinets, shelves, and so on. Various benefits of recycled cardboard waste also have a positive impact. We can hone our creativity and reduce the accumulation of trash.



Cardboard Waste Observation

Waste is a substance or material that is no longer used and has a negative impact on the environment or health. Waste can be managed in the 3R (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle) way. In this observation, I will focus on cardboard waste and the benefits of managing cardboard waste because if this principle is not applied, the environment will be dirty and polluted.

Cardboard waste is a type of waste that comes from rice wrappers, used plastic, foam, and also other used goods. Cardboard boxes vary in type and also size, there are large, small, and medium ones. The colors also vary, some are brown, red, white, and others.

Cardboard waste is a thick paper waste. Cardboard waste is one of the causes of the increasing amount of waste in disposal sites. By managing cardboard waste, we can help maintain a clean environment.

The benefit of cardboard waste is, for example, making a table from the waste. This table can be used for learning or as a place to eat. In this observation, I applied the principle of Reuse or Recycling, because the recycling process turns it into something clean.



Cardboard Waste Observation

Waste is an item or substance that is no longer used. Waste is generated from industrial factories or nature. Waste can be in solid, liquid, or gas form, which can cause negative impacts on nature and the surrounding environment. Proper waste management must be carried out to create a healthy environment. In this observation, I will explain the results of my observation on used cardboard.

Cardboard waste is solid waste made from thick paper material. Cardboard usually comes from used industrial packaging, electronics, or toys. Cardboard itself is often easily damaged because it is made from recycled paper.

Cardboard has characteristics such as being brown or white, having a light and fragile texture, and can be in the form of sheets or boxes.Although it is waste, cardboard can be reused through the recycling process, which can reduce the amount that ends up in landfills.

The benefits of cardboard waste are that it can be used for highvalue handicrafts. It can also be utilized as a compost ingredient to make plants more fertile. The environmental benefits include reducing greenhouse gases and saving forests from deforestation, as cardboard is made from paper, which in turn comes from trees.
